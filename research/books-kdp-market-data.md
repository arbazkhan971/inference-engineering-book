## Key facts — bullets, every claim dated and sourced

**Categories used by AI bestsellers (Amazon.com, checked 2026-08-27):**
- AI book bestseller lists live under Computers & Technology nodes: "Artificial Intelligence" (node 491300) and "Natural Language Processing" (node 271581011). [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/gp/bestsellers/books/491300), [Amazon NLP](https://www.amazon.com/gp/bestsellers/books/271581011)
- A distinct "Generative AI" node (211759007011) exists — newer, and dominated by KDP/self-pub titles (e.g., "Claude AI for Beginners Bible", "The ChatGPT Millionaire"). [Amazon](https://us.amazon.com/Best-Sellers-Generative-AI/zgbs/books/211759007011)
- AI-node bestseller mix (2026-08-27 snapshot): trade/business AI books (Co-Intelligence, Superintelligence, The Nvidia Way) plus practitioner titles ("30 Agents Every AI Engineer Must Build") and low-cost reference guides; technical deep-dives are a minority of the top-20. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/gp/bestsellers/books/491300)
- Hot new releases in the node are practitioner/project titles ("50 ML Projects To Understand LLMs", Sybex "CompTIA SecAI+ Study Guide"). [Amazon new releases](https://www.amazon.com/gp/new-releases/books/491300)

**Rank vs sales estimates (BSR → sales, US Kindle store; curve-fits only — Amazon publishes no sales data):**
- BSR #1,000 ≈ 70-180 sales/day; #10,000 ≈ 13-25/day; #50,000 ≈ 3-15/day; #100,000 ≈ 1-2/day; past #500,000 rank stops tracking sales usefully. [ManuscriptReport](https://manuscriptreport.com/tools/book-sales-calculator)
- Example: BSR #25,000 → ~$965/mo estimated Kindle royalty. [Profitable.app](https://profitable.app/tools/kdp-sales-calculator)
- Print BSR follows a lower-velocity curve than Kindle BSR; KU borrows also move BSR, so raw rank can overstate paid-unit sales. [Boocu](https://boocu.com/tools/bsr-sales-estimator.html), [Kindlepreneur](https://kindlepreneur.com/amazon-kdp-sales-rank-calculator/)
- BSR updates hourly and reflects recent velocity only; any single snapshot is an order-of-magnitude guide. [Profitable.app](https://profitable.app/tools/kdp-sales-calculator)

## Coverage map — what exists, how deep
- Dense: AI-literacy trade books, prompt-engineering and "make money with AI" KDP titles, beginner guides.
- Strong but recent: LLM fundamentals and applied LLM engineering (Raschka, LLM Engineer's Handbook, Huyen) — all Oct-Dec 2024; 2025-2026 updates/second editions exist but were not individually verified here.
- Thin: inference/serving engineering specifically (quantization, KV-cache, latency/throughput, GPU economics) — no dedicated title appeared in the AI or NLP bestseller nodes reviewed 2026-08-27. Hedge: niche titles may sell steadily without charting.

## Series angle — what this means for Inference Engineering Vol. II positioning
- Price anchor: $59.99 is the established practitioner list price (Manning/Packt); O'Reilly pushed to ~$79.99 for flagships. $55-70 list is in-market.
- Length anchor: 370-530 pp covers comparable successes; Vol. II need not exceed ~500 pp.
- Categories: professional titles dual-list in "AI & ML" + "NLP"; the cheaper "Generative AI" node is KDP self-pub territory.
- Sales reality: niche technical books typically sustain ranks in the tens of thousands (≈3-15 sales/day Kindle-equivalent) — steady backlist, not spike bestsellers.
