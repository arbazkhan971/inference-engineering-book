### Findings

1. **[P1]** — Checkpoint 4 answer key, manuscript/17-cache-aware-harness-design.md:226 — the shared-plan arithmetic double-counts the first child's preamble read and contradicts 17.5's own formula.
   - Exact quoted text: `*(Shared: 1.25·20K write + 10 × (0.1·20K read + 6K fresh) = 25K + 10 × 8K = 105K-units. Isolated: 10 × 1.25 × 26K = 325K-units. The fleet shape is ~3.1× cheaper — derived; on Anthropic, stagger the first child's write before the other nine fire.)*`
   - Replacement: `*(Shared: the first child pays 1.25·20K to write the preamble + 6K fresh; the other nine pay 0.1·20K read + 6K fresh each: 25K + 6K + 9 × 8K = 103K-units. Isolated: 10 × 1.25 × 26K = 325K-units. The fleet shape is ~3.2× cheaper — derived; on Anthropic, stagger the first child's write before the other nine fire.)*`
   - Why: the child whose request performs the 1.25× write is not also billed a 0.1× read of the same 20K tokens (no provider bills write+read on one request's prefix), and the chapter's own 17.5 formula — `1.25 + 0.1·(N−1)` (research/subagent-context-isolation-cache.md) — has no read term for child 1. Correct total is 103K (not 105K) and the ratio is 325/103 ≈ 3.2× (not 3.1×). An incorrect answer key in a book whose promise is re-derivable arithmetic; must be fixed before release.

2. **[P2]** — §17.4 "The arithmetic of coming back" — fuzzy arithmetic in the durable money spine.
   - Exact quoted text: `The premium buys ten turns' worth of tenth-price reads, which is why it exists; at resume time it is a lump that scales with the transcript.`
   - Replacement: `The first hit repays it — one 0.9× discount against the 0.25× surcharge — which is why it exists; at resume time it is a lump that scales with the transcript.`
   - Why: the sentence's antecedent "premium" is the 25% surcharge (0.25×), which equals 2.5 tenth-price reads, not ten; even read charitably as the whole 1.25× write, the figure is 12.5 reads, not ten. Chapter 14's own break-even (~1.1 reuses, "after two cache reads" at the 1-hour price) makes "ten turns' worth" look like wrong math to a careful reader.
