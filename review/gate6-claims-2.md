## Findings

**[P2] Appendix B, B.3 "The cache loop" card — `N` is used in two incompatible senses on one card.**
- Location: `manuscript/appendix-b-arithmetic-cheatsheet.md` (~line 82–88).
- Derivation: the card's cost line — "the first request writes, the rest read: w + (N − 1)·r, versus N uncached" — defines N as *total requests* (N−1 readers). Under that definition break-even solves w + (N−1)r < N ⟺ N > (w−r)/(1−r) = (1.25−0.1)/0.9 = **1.28** (2.11 at w = 2). The card's next line — "break-even reuses: N ≥ (w − 1)/(1 − r)" → **0.28** (1.11 at w = 2) — defines N as *reuses after the write*, whose correct cost line is w + N·r vs N + 1 uncached (1.25 + 0.1N < N + 1 ⟺ N > 0.278). A reader plugging N = 0.28 into the printed cost expression gets a negative read count.
- Conflict: internal to the appendix B card; chapter 14 §14.3 is self-consistent (it defines N as reuses and never prints the (N−1) form). Printed conclusions ("one reuse pays the premium"; 1-hour "after two cache reads") are correct under the reuses reading. Related wording nit carried by ch. 14 checkpoint answer 1 and appendix B: "N ≥ 1.11 reuses, i.e. two uses total" — the 1-hour premium breaks even after two *reads*, i.e. three requests total; "two uses total" misleads if "uses" is read as requests. Smallest fix: restate the cost line as "w + N·r, versus N + 1 uncached (N = reuses)" and gloss the 1-hour case as "after two cache reads (three requests)."

**[P2] Ch. 14 §14.3 — "0.1 on the newest generation of every major provider" contradicted by the chapter's own §14.2 table.**
- Location: `manuscript/14-the-cache-that-pays-your-bill.md` §14.3 (read price r sentence) vs §14.2 DeepSeek row/bullet.

Smallest fix: "0.1 on the newest generation of the US majors; DeepSeek's newest rows sit at 2–3% — read your model's row."

**[P2] 1-hour-write crossover constant: 1.6 (ch. 14, appendix B) vs ≈1.74 (appendix B parenthetical, ch. 17).**
- Location: `manuscript/14-the-cache-that-pays-your-bill.md` §14.3 ("crossover 2.0/1.25 = 1.6 rewrites"); `manuscript/appendix-b-arithmetic-cheatsheet.md` B.3 expiry card (quotes both 1.6 and the inequality); `manuscript/17-cache-aware-harness-design.md` §17.4/checkpoint 2.

Both round to the same actionable threshold (≥2 gaps/hour; ch. 14's "about twice an hour" and ch. 17's N ≥ 2 agree), so no budgeting error results — but the two quoted crossover constants differ by exactly the omitted read term.
- Conflict: ch. 14/appendix B headline constant vs appendix B's own parenthetical and ch. 17's inequality. Smallest fix: one clause — "1.6 ignoring reads, ~1.74 counting them; either way two gaps per hour justify the premium."
